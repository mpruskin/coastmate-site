Delete your account

In the app: Settings, Account, Delete Account. There are two steps.

  1. Step 1 is a warning. If you have a trip in progress, it says that deleting the account stops all alerts to your shore contact for that trip.
  2. Step 2: type DELETE and tap the red button.

What happens next:

  • The server deletes your data first: your login and email address, your vessel and boater profile (including the saved shore contact), the memory CoastMate™ keeps about you, Departure Check answers, records that a question was blocked, float plan records and your uploaded trip photos.
  • If the server delete does not finish, nothing is removed from the phone and your account is kept.
  • After that the app wipes the phone: float plans, Past Plans, Trip Log trips, incident reports, the maintenance log, trip photos, settings, the event log, the saved sign-in, reminders and alarms, and background recording. It reads the storage back to check.
  • This cannot be undone, and the data cannot be recovered.
  • Your data is deleted right away from our systems. Copies held by our service providers are removed on their own schedules, which we ask them to keep to 30 days or less.
  • Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription. A subscription is cancelled in the App Store or Google Play.
  • Emails already sent to your shore contacts cannot be recalled.

Sign out

In the app: Settings, Account, Sign Out. Signing out stops syncing. The data on the phone and the cloud copy stay as they were.

Change your email address

In the app: Settings, Account, Change Email. Enter the new address and Save. The app then says: Check your new email to confirm.

Change or reset your password

  • Signed in: Settings, Account, Change Password.
  • Signed out: on the sign-in screen, Forgot password?, then enter your email. CoastMate™ sends a link. Opening the link on the phone with CoastMate™ installed opens the new password screen.

Where your data is stored

  • Float plans, float plan history, Trip Log trips, and settings are stored on the phone.
  • When you are signed in, your vessel profiles and boater profile (which includes the saved shore contact) are copied to your account, and trip photos are uploaded to your account.
  • Without an account the app works offline and stores everything on the phone.
  • A float plan email goes to the shore contact address you enter, and to your Back up Contact if you add one. That copy is in the recipient's mailbox.
