CoastMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

OceanComm Labs LLC, a Massachusetts limited liability company with its address at 1 Lazy Lane, Harwich, MA 02645 ("CoastMate", "we", "our", "us"), receives, collects, stores and manages users' personal data in accordance with this Privacy Policy. We treat our users' privacy the way we would like to be treated ourselves.

This Privacy Policy explains how CoastMate collects and uses personal data when you use the CoastMate mobile application, the CoastMate live location page, the coastmate.ai website and related services (the "App" and the "Services").

CoastMate is offered in the United States. Text messages are sent to US phone numbers only.

Information for California, Nevada and Virginia residents: please see the sections "Additional Information for California Residents", "Additional Information for Nevada Residents" and "Additional Information for Virginia Residents" below.

1. Data Collected

1.1 Data that you provide to us

1.1.1 You provide personal data when you create an account, create or file a float plan, enter your vessel details, add a shore contact, ask the CoastMate Voice Agent a question, take or save trip photos, use the logbook, contact us, or otherwise use the App.

1.1.2 The personal data you provide may include:

  • your email address and password (your password is handled by our authentication provider);
  • your vessel profile, such as vessel name, registration details and equipment dates you enter;
  • float plan details, such as departure and return times, destination, route, people aboard and trip notes;
  • the names, email addresses and phone numbers of your shore contacts (see 1.1.4);
  • the questions you speak or type to the CoastMate Voice Agent, and the answers the App gives;
  • logbook entries, pre-departure checklist answers and Departure Check records;
  • trip photos you choose to save, and the location where each was taken, when known;
  • your app preferences and onboarding choices;
  • your subscription status, when paid plans are available.

1.1.3 Location. With your permission, the App collects your device's precise location. When you are on a trip, the App can record your position in the background, including when the screen is locked, so your shore contact can see where you are. On iPhone the App asks for "Always" location permission and shows the system's location indicator. You can turn location off in your phone's settings; some features will not work without it.

1.1.4 Shore contacts. You provide the name, email address and phone number of each shore contact, either by typing them or by choosing a person with your phone's contact picker. If you use the picker, we receive only the name, phone number and email address you select; the App does not read the rest of your contacts. We use them only to send the messages described in this Privacy Policy, such as your float plan, alerts, and the live location link. You are responsible for having your contact's permission to give us their details. Shore contacts have not created an account with us. A shore contact can stop texts by replying STOP, and can stop emails or ask us to remove their details by contacting us at support@coastmate.ai.

1.1.5 Battery level. The App reads your phone's battery level and whether it is charging, and includes it in messages and on the live location page so your shore contact knows whether your phone may run out of power.

1.2 Data that we collect automatically

1.2.1 We automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, device and operating system type, app version, language, the date and time of access, and diagnostic information needed to run the Services.

1.2.2 When the App opens, it checks for app updates through our update provider. When you use certain features, the App sends your location to a weather provider and to a place-lookup service to show weather and place names, and Nearby Services and the local VHF channel search send your location to our AI provider.

1.2.3 We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, attribution or tracking tools in the App. We do not use advertising identifiers.

1.2.4 When someone opens a live location link, our systems and our map provider receive that visitor's IP address, device and browser information, the time, and the area of the map viewed. We do not use cookies or local storage on the live location page. The page and its link are removed as soon as you end the trip.

1.2.5 What the Voice Agent remembers. If you are signed in, the App keeps a count of the general subjects you ask the Voice Agent about (such as weather, anchoring or safety equipment) and of the places you name in your float plans, along with the date each was last seen. It does not keep your questions. When a subject or place comes up often, it is included with your question when we send it to our AI provider, so answers fit you better, for example by explaining the basics less. Places also appear as suggestions when you plan a trip. You can see this list and clear it in Settings. It is deleted when you delete your account.

1.3 Data from third-party sources

1.3.1 When paid plans are available we will receive your subscription status and purchase identifiers from the Apple App Store or Google Play (and our subscription service). We do not receive your payment card number.

1.4 Special categories of personal data

1.4.1 We do not intentionally collect health, biometric or other special categories of personal data. Precise location is described in 1.1.3 and in the California section below. If you ask the CoastMate Voice Agent a question about alcohol, drugs or impairment, the App answers with a fixed safety message, does not send your question to our AI provider, and records only that a question was blocked, not your words.

2. Minors

2.1 Our Services are not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Users aged 13 or older who have not reached the age of majority in their state may use the Services with permission from a parent or legal guardian. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will delete it.

3. Use of the Data

3.1 We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • to provide the Services you requested, including your float plan, the logbook, the pre-departure checklist, weather, the CoastMate Voice Agent, and the TideMate and AnchorMate alarms;
  • to send your shore contacts your float plan, alerts, texts and emails when your trip is overdue, and a link to a live location page showing your boat's name, position, track, battery level, last update, departure time and expected return time;
  • to send alerts and notifications to your phone, including alerts when you are past your return time;
  • to answer your questions with the help of our AI provider and to speak the answers aloud;
  • to bill and collect payment for paid plans, when available;
  • to communicate with you about the App and Services and provide customer support;
  • to keep the Services secure, fix problems, and prevent misuse;
  • to enforce our Terms and applicable law and to protect the rights and safety of our users and others;
  • to meet legal requirements and respond to lawful requests by public authorities;
  • to transfer your information in the case of a sale, merger, consolidation, liquidation, reorganization or acquisition.

3.2 We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3.3 We do not carry out solely automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects about you. The CoastMate Voice Agent uses artificial intelligence to answer questions. Its answers are general information, not a substitute for your own judgment, the law, or the Coast Guard, your state's boating rules, or any local or municipal rules that apply where you boat.

4. Sharing of Data

4.1 We share personal data only where necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. We do not sell your personal information.

4.2 CoastMate is operated from the United States. Your personal data is processed in the United States, and may also be processed in other countries where our service providers operate.

4.3 Your shore contacts and the live location page

4.3.1 When you file a float plan, we send it to the shore contacts you name. If you turn on "Share live location page with my shore contacts" (available on Pro and during the free trial), your shore contacts also receive a link to a live location page.

4.3.2 The live location page shows your boat's name, position, track, battery level, the time of the last update, your departure time and your expected return time, and nothing else. The link is long and cannot be guessed. It stops working when you end the trip. Anyone who has the link can open the page, and your shore contact can forward it. We ask that it be shared only with people who need it.

4.3.3 On Pro and during the free trial, if your trip is overdue, we send your shore contacts emails and texts (texts go only to shore contacts who have replied YES, see 4.3.4). Each text includes the link and your battery level. Texts are sent to US phone numbers only.

4.3.4 Text messages. We send text messages (SMS) to your shore contacts only for your trip: one intro text when you add the contact and turn on texts, which asks the contact to reply YES to get alerts for your trips; then, only if the contact has replied YES, up to four texts if your trip is overdue (each includes the live location link and your battery level), and one text to let them know you are safe when the trip ends safely. By adding a shore contact's phone number and turning on texts for your trip, you confirm that the person has agreed to receive these texts. Message frequency: one intro text (you can send it again, once a day, if the contact has not replied), up to four overdue texts for a late trip, and one all-clear text. Message and data rates may apply. To get alerts, reply YES to the intro text. To stop texts, reply STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact us at support@coastmate.ai. Texts are sent to US phone numbers only.

4.3.5 No sharing of mobile information. We do not share mobile phone numbers or text message consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We share a phone number only with our text message provider so that it can deliver the message. We do not send marketing texts.

4.4 Service providers

4.4.1 We share personal information with companies that perform tasks on our behalf (our "Service Providers"). We share only the information reasonably necessary for them to provide their services. Our Service Providers include:

  1. Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage and server functions): stores your account, profile, float plans, location trail and trip photos.
  2. Anthropic (artificial intelligence): receives the questions you ask the CoastMate Voice Agent and the context needed to answer them. When you use Nearby Services or the local VHF channel search, it also receives your location, and for the channel search the vessel and home port details you entered. It may search the web on our behalf. The App asks your permission before the first question is sent, and you can change your choice in Settings. If you decline, the App still sends a message that sounds like an emergency, so it can point you to VHF Channel 16 or 911.
  3. ElevenLabs (text to speech): receives the text the App speaks aloud, such as answers, alerts and confirmations. It can include trip details such as places and times.
  4. Voyage AI (search): receives the text of your question to find relevant boating information.
  5. Resend (email): delivers our emails, including float plans and alerts, to you and your shore contacts.
  6. Twilio (text messages): delivers text messages to your shore contacts.
  7. Google (maps and Android services): provides map images in emails, the map on the live location page, and map and notification components used on Android. Google receives the map areas requested and, for the live location page, the visitor's IP address.
  8. Expo (app updates): the App checks for updates when it opens.
  9. National Weather Service and NOAA (weather, tides and buoys), Open-Meteo (air quality and place search) and Photon (place lookup, operated by komoot): receive your location, or a place you type, to show weather, conditions and place names.
  10. Apple App Store and Google Play: handle downloads and, when available, subscriptions.
  11. Your phone's speech service (Apple or Google): converts your voice into text on your device or through Apple's or Google's servers, under their privacy policies.

4.4.2 Data sent to these providers is processed under their own terms and privacy policies. We take reasonable steps to limit what we send to what is needed. Each provider keeps data for as long as its own policy says, and we cannot guarantee how long.

5. Safeguarding Your Personal Data

5.1 The protection of your personal data is vital to us. We take commercially reasonable measures to protect your personal data from loss, misuse, and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration and destruction, considering the risks involved and the nature of the data. Our contractors and business partners may access personal data only when needed and under a duty of confidentiality. We use encryption when data travels between the App, the live location page and our servers. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5.2 You agree to protect the security of your login information and not to give it to any third party.

5.3 If you become aware of any unauthorized use of your account, you must notify us immediately at support@coastmate.ai.

6. Access to Your Personal Data

6.1 You may view and correct much of your data in the App. Subject to applicable law, you may also:

  • request deletion of some or all of the personal data we hold about you;
  • request correction of your personal data;
  • request access to, and a copy of, your personal data;
  • ask us to limit the use of your personal data.

6.2 Deleting your account. You can delete your account in the App under Settings. Deleting your account removes your account and all your data from your phone and from our systems, including your float plans, logbook, vessel profile, trip photos, location trail, live location links, scheduled messages, and records of blocked questions, and the subjects and places the Voice Agent remembers. This cannot be undone and there is no recovery. The App asks you to confirm in two steps so you do not delete by accident. You can ask us for a copy of your data under Section 6.3, and you can export your logbook from the App before you delete.

6.3 To view, change or remove data associated with your account in another way, contact us at support@coastmate.ai. We respond without undue delay and within 45 days, which we may extend once by up to 45 days when needed. We do not charge a fee for ordinary requests.

6.4 Shore contacts. If you are a shore contact and want to stop receiving messages or want your details removed, reply STOP to a text, or contact us at support@coastmate.ai.

7. Changes to the Privacy Policy

7.1 We may amend this Privacy Policy from time to time. Use of information we collect is subject to the Privacy Policy in effect at the time the information is used. If we make major changes in the way we collect or use information, we will notify you by posting an announcement in the App or sending you an email.

8. Merger or Sale of the Company

8.1 If CoastMate merges with or is acquired by another company, or sells all or a portion of its assets, your personal data may be disclosed to our advisers and any prospective purchaser or the purchaser's advisers and may be among the assets transferred. Personal data will remain subject to this Privacy Policy.

9. Data Retention

9.1 We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active, unless you delete it earlier. Your float plans, logbook, location trail and trip photos stay in your account until you delete them or delete your account.

9.2 Live location links. A live location link stops working when you end the trip. The stored location trail remains in your account until you delete it or your account, as described above.

9.3 Account deletion. When you delete your account, we delete your data from our systems right away. Copies held by our service providers (for example in backups or logs) are removed on their own schedules, which we ask them to keep to 30 days or less. We cannot guarantee this period for every provider.

9.4 We may keep information where the law requires it or where it is needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

9.5 We process information in the United States. Laws there may differ from the laws of your own state.

10. Additional Information for California Residents

10.1 This section describes how we handle the personal information of California residents under the California Consumer Privacy Act ("CCPA"). It covers personal information we collected, shared or used during the last 12 months.

10.2 We describe the personal information we collect in Section 1, "Data Collected", and how we use it in Section 3, "Use of the Data". We share certain personal information as described in Section 4, "Sharing of Data". We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

10.3 Precise geolocation. Your device's precise location is sensitive personal information under California law. We collect it only to provide the Services you ask for, such as showing your position to your shore contacts and showing weather and place names. We do not use it to infer other characteristics about you.

10.4 CoastMate does not sell or share the personal information of any user, including users under 16. Our minimum age is 13, as described in Section 2.

10.5 In accordance with the CCPA, California residents are entitled, in addition to the rights described in this Privacy Policy, to:

  • Request deletion. You may request deletion of personal information we collect from you;
  • Request correction. You may ask us to correct inaccurate personal information;
  • Request a copy. You may request a copy of the personal information we hold in your account, such as your profile, float plans, shore contacts, logbook, location trail and photos. We do not store recordings of your voice or the text of your questions in our own database, although the providers in Section 4.4 receive your questions to answer them, and we keep no copy on our servers;
  • Designate an agent. You may designate an authorized agent to make requests on your behalf.

10.6 To exercise your rights, contact us at support@coastmate.ai.

10.7 CoastMate does not tolerate discrimination. The quality of our Services is equal for all users, whether or not you exercise your rights under the CCPA.

11. Additional Information for Nevada Residents
